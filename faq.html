FAQ

Answer the questions that usually slow down a booking decision.

How do I book?

Each trip page should include a Book Now button that links directly to your WeTravel package.

What is included?

List accommodations, hosted activities, transportation, and meals on each trip details page.

Do flights come with the trip?

For most group trips, international airfare is separate unless you explicitly include it.

Can I ask questions before booking?

Yes. Your contact page should make it easy for travelers to send an inquiry before they pay.
