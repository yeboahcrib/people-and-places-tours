## What's actually required, not just expected

### 1. Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) — a real compliance action, not just a webpage

Ghana has an actual data protection law with teeth. Key points from the
Data Protection Commission:

- Any organization that collects personal data (names, emails, phone
  numbers, payment details — all of which People & Places already collects
  via the contact form and WhatsApp) must **register as a data controller
  with the Data Protection Commission (DPC)**, and renew that registration
  every two years.
- A privacy policy/notice, consent language, and (per DPC guidance) a data
  protection impact assessment are expected as part of compliance.
- Data must be collected for a specific, stated purpose, processed fairly
  and transparently, secured appropriately, and breaches must be reported
  to the DPC and affected individuals.
- Non-compliance carries fines and potential criminal liability.

**Action item for founders, separate from any website copy:** confirm
whether People & Places is already registered as a data controller with the
DPC. If not, this is a real registration task, not something a privacy
policy page satisfies by itself.

### 2. Baseline pages expected by any consumer-facing booking business

Regardless of Ghana-specific law, given the site takes names, emails,
phone numbers and (eventually) payment details from guests in the US, UK,
Canada and elsewhere:

- **Privacy Policy** — what's collected, why, who it's shared with, how
  long it's kept, and what rights a visitor has.
- **Terms & Conditions / Booking Policy** — what a booking means, payment
  terms, what happens if either side needs to cancel or change plans,
  traveler responsibilities, liability limits.
- **Cancellation & Refund Policy** — can be its own page or a section of
  the Terms; the roadmap treats it as a named requirement, so it's split
  out below for clarity.

The current site has none of these — the Contact page's privacy/terms
links point to `#` (confirmed in the Sprint 0 audit).

## Inspiration reviewed

Three comparable diaspora/Africa-travel operators were reviewed for
structure and tone (their exact wording is not reused — only the shape of
what they cover):

- [Sorted Chale](https://sortedchale.com/privacy) — Ghana-based Africa
  travel agency. Clean, numbered privacy policy (~14 sections), plain
  "In Short:" summaries before each detailed section, names its actual
  vendors (Paystack, Hubtel, Google Drive, Wix) rather than speaking in the
  abstract.
- [TravelMo](https://www.travelmo.com/terms-and-conditions) — US-based,
  Africa-focused. Heavier, more defensive terms and conditions: strict
  24-hour refund window, mandatory arbitration clause, broad liability
  waivers, perpetual photography release.
- [Buoyant Travel](https://buoyanttravel.com/terms-conditions) — US-based,
  diaspora-focused group trips. Similar defensive posture: 48-hour
  non-refundable cutoff, explicit "no chargebacks" clause, $495 planning
  fee retained on company-side cancellations, jurisdiction locked to
  Maryland courts.

**Deliberate choice for People & Places:** TravelMo and Buoyant Travel's
tone (mandatory arbitration, no-exceptions chargeback bans, perpetual
uncompensated photo rights) reads as legally aggressive in a way that cuts
against the Brand Foundation's voice principles — "sound like a thoughtful
host," not a corporate risk department. The draft below borrows their
*structure* (deposit terms, force majeure, liability limits, traveler
responsibilities) but keeps language plainer and less adversarial, and uses
Ghana as the governing jurisdiction (since the company is genuinely
Ghana-based) rather than routing disputes through a foreign arbitration
clause that would be impractical for an international guest to use anyway.

## Draft: Privacy Policy

> **Last updated:** [date] · **Effective for:** peopleandplaces.com (or
> final production domain)

### 1. Who we are

People & Places ("we," "us," "our") is a Ghana-founded tour company based
in Accra, serving guests across Ghana. This policy explains what
information we collect when you visit our website, contact us, or book a
tour with us, and what we do with it.

### 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us directly:**
- Name, email address, phone number/WhatsApp number
- Trip details you share with us (dates, group size, interests, questions)
- Proof of payment (e.g. a bank transfer or Mobile Money receipt) when you
  book a tour. Payments are currently made directly to us by bank transfer
  or Mobile Money — we do not collect or store card details through our
  website, since booking requests and payment are handled manually rather
  than through an automated payment processor at this time

**Information collected automatically:**
- Basic technical information (browser type, device, IP address) and
  cookies used for analytics, if analytics tools are in use

### 3. How we use your information

- To respond to your inquiry and plan your tour
- To process payments and manage your booking
- To send you information you've asked for (trip details, confirmations)
- To improve our website and services

We do not sell your personal information.

### 4. Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us operate:

- Your bank or Mobile Money provider (MTN, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo), when
  you send us a payment directly — we don't route payments through a
  separate third-party processor at this time
- [Website hosting/CMS provider, once selected]
- WhatsApp/Meta, if you contact us via WhatsApp — subject to their own
  privacy terms
- Any local partners directly involved in delivering your specific tour
  (e.g. a driver or guide who needs your name and pickup details)

We do not share your information with anyone else without your consent,
except where required by law.

### 5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to understand how visitors use the site and to
remember basic preferences. You can control cookies through your browser
settings.

### 6. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services and
meet legal or tax obligations, and then delete or anonymize it.

### 7. Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), and applicable law where
you live, you can ask us to:

- Tell you what personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your information, where we're not required to keep it
- Withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time

To make any of these requests, contact us at [email].

### 8. Children

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect
information from anyone under 18.

### 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when
it was last revised.

### 10. Contact us

## Draft: Terms & Conditions / Booking Policy

### 1. Booking a tour

Choose a tour and submit a booking request through our website. We'll
confirm availability and send you payment instructions directly — a
booking is confirmed once we've agreed on your tour details and received
your payment. Day tours can be booked for any day with advance notice —
this is not a guaranteed daily-departure schedule, and confirmation depends
on availability.

### 2. Payment

Booking requests and payment are currently handled directly between you
and our team, rather than through an automated online payment system. Once
your request is confirmed, we'll send you our bank transfer or Mobile Money
details along with a booking reference. Please include that reference with
your payment and send us a confirmation (a screenshot or receipt works
fine) so we can match it to your booking.

- **Deposit:** 30% of the total tour price, due at booking to confirm your
  reservation. This deposit is non-refundable once paid.
- **Balance:** For tailored multi-day and custom tours, the remaining 70%
  is due 30 days before your tour start date.
- **Day tours:** Full payment is due at the time of booking. If you need to
  cancel, you'll receive a full refund if you let us know at least 48 hours
  before your tour; cancellations inside 48 hours are non-refundable.
- **Accepted payment methods:** bank transfer, Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone
  Cash, AirtelTigo), and major credit/debit cards. [To be reconfirmed as
  accurate.]
- Prices are shown in US dollars unless stated otherwise.

### 3. If you need to cancel

**Tailored multi-day and custom tours:** Your deposit is non-refundable
from the time it's paid. If you cancel within 30 days of your tour start
date, the full amount paid (deposit and any balance) is non-refundable.

**Day tours:** Cancel at least 48 hours before your tour for a full refund.
Cancellations inside 48 hours are non-refundable.

We understand plans change — talk to us as early as possible, and we'll
always try to find a workable solution, such as rescheduling where that's
possible.

### 4. If we need to change or cancel your tour

We will only change or cancel a confirmed tour for genuine operational
reasons — safety, weather, government restrictions, or other circumstances
outside our reasonable control ("force majeure"). If this happens, we will
offer you a rescheduled date or a full refund.

### 5. Your responsibilities as a traveler

You're responsible for arranging any visa, valid passport, and recommended
vaccinations for travel to Ghana. We're happy to share general guidance,
but we're not a substitute for official embassy or health advice.

### 6. Health, accessibility and comfort

Tell us about any accessibility needs, health considerations, or comfort
preferences when you book, so we can plan accordingly. We do everything we
reasonably can to accommodate you, but we can't guarantee every site or
activity is fully accessible — some heritage sites in particular have
physical limitations outside our control.

### 7. Photography and video

We may take photos or video during your tour to share on our website and
social media. We'll always ask before using anything that clearly shows
you or your group, and you're welcome to opt out at any time.

### 8. Liability

While we take care in selecting drivers, guides, accommodations and
partners, we are not responsible for the independent acts of third-party
providers (hotels, airlines, restaurants, etc.) outside our direct control.
Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be excluded under
Ghanaian law.

### 9. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Ghana.

### 10. Contact us

Questions about a booking or these terms: +233 50 367 3473 ·
[email] · Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.
