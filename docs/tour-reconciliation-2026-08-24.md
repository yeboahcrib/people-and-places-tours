## 6. Group sizes

The document states a minimum of three for pricing on every tour, and for
**Ada** states availability as three or more. Ada is now 3–15; it was 1–15.

The other day tours keep a minimum of one on purpose. A solo traveller can
book them at $20–30 more, and showing "3–8 People" would read as a refusal to
the travellers most likely to be booking alone.

Local Experiences require **four**, per the document.
