How It Began

From Passion
to Purpose

People & Places began with a frustration we kept running into. Visitors were seeing Ghana from the outside: through hotel windows, through bus glass, never quite close enough to meet anyone.

We are Ghanaians with roots across Accra, Cape Coast, and the Volta Region, so we knew what they were missing. In 2021 we started hosting the trips we wished those visitors had been on.

Since then we have hosted more than 300 guests. What we hear most often is not that Ghana was beautiful, though it is. It is that they felt looked after.
